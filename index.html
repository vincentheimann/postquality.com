Before you install What you need

Post Quality runs on your own accounts, so you hold the keys. Check these three first.

  • Your own n8n

    The workflow tool your reviews run in. Self-hosted; your site and n8n must reach each other. No n8n yet? How to get n8n.

  • An Anthropic API key

    The setup guide checks it once and stores it in n8n, never in the plugin.

  • Your own AI bill

    You pay Anthropic per review and your n8n hosting. Cap it with a spend limit in your Anthropic account. What it costs.

  • WordPress 6.6+
  • PHP 8.0+
  • Single site, no Multisite

A good fit if you run your own WordPress and can follow a setup guide for n8n. Not a fit if you want a hosted service with nothing to set up.

How it works Three steps to your first score

  1. 1

    Connect your accounts

    The setup guide asks for three values: your n8n address, an n8n API key and your Anthropic key. It builds the workflow and tests the connection both ways.

  2. 2

    Review a post

    Click Review on a post, or select several at once. Your site sends each one to your own n8n workflow, which asks Anthropic's models.

  3. 3

    Fix what matters first

    The score, the issues and the suggested edits come back into WP Admin. The Dashboard ranks what to fix next.
