# Post Quality Manager

**Score every post, see what to fix next and keep your back-catalog sharp.** AI content reviews, stored and tracked in WP Admin.

As a blog grows, its back-catalog quietly ages: older posts drift out of date, quality varies from one article to the next, and re-reading everything by hand does not scale. SEO plugins score keywords and metadata, but nothing tells you which posts need editorial attention, and why.

Post Quality Manager scores every post on four universal pillars, **Structure, Readability, Engagement and Credibility**, keeps the history, and shows at a glance where to act next. Niche tailoring lives in a Blog Profile and your own Editorial Standards, never in code, so the same plugin serves a marketing blog, a technical blog or a lifestyle site.

## How it works

Your WordPress site sends a post to **your own n8n workflow**, the workflow asks the AI provider you chose, and the result comes back into WP Admin: a score, the issues found, suggested edits, key takeaways, sources, SEO and audit notes. You bring the n8n instance and the AI provider account; the plugin brings the dashboard, the workflow and the agents.
