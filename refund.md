_Last updated 2026-10-09. Published at https://postquality.com/refund._

If Post Quality Manager Pro is not what you needed, you get your money back.

- **14 days, no questions asked.** Ask within 14 days of your purchase and the full amount is refunded.
- **How to ask.** Open the customer portal link in your purchase email and click Refund, or write to hello@postquality.com with your license email. Freemius, the seller of record, processes the refund to your original payment method, usually within a few business days.
- **What happens next.** The license is cancelled and your site returns to the free tier. Your stored reviews and settings stay on your site.
- **Renewals.** A renewal payment can be refunded within 14 days of the renewal date on the same terms.
- **After 14 days.** No refund is due, but you can cancel the renewal at any time so you are not charged again.

The free tier is the trial: try Review and Key Takeaways before you buy.
