## Free plugin

Questions and bug reports for the free plugin go to the WordPress.org support forum: https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/post-quality-manager/. The forum is answered on a best-effort basis; bugs confirmed there are fixed in a following release.

## Pro customers

Pro licenses include email support at **hello@postquality.com**. Write from the email address of your purchase and you get an answer within **2 business days** (Monday to Friday). Include the plugin and workflow versions, your WordPress and PHP versions, and what you did before the problem; the plugin's n8n Workflow page shows the versions after a test ping.

Support covers the plugin and the n8n workflow template it ships: installing, connecting n8n, using the features, and bugs. It does not cover running your n8n instance, your AI provider account, your hosting, or custom changes to the workflow, though a pointer in the right direction is always offered.

## Security problems

Report a security problem privately, never on the forum: see https://github.com/vincentheimann/post-quality-manager/security/policy or write to hello@postquality.com.
