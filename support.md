_Last updated 2026-10-23. Published at https://postquality.com/support._

## Free plugin

Post Quality Manager is not installable yet; [leave your email](mailto:hello@postquality.com?subject=Tell%20me%20when%20Post%20Quality%20Manager%20is%20available) and you will hear once, when you can install it.

Until the plugin is listed in the WordPress.org plugin directory, questions and bug reports for the free plugin go to hello@postquality.com; once it is listed, they go to its support forum there. They are answered on a best-effort basis; confirmed bugs are fixed in a following release.

## Pro customers

Pro licenses include email support at **hello@postquality.com**. Write from the email address of your purchase and you get an answer within **5 business days** (Monday to Friday). Include the plugin and workflow versions, your WordPress and PHP versions, and what you did before the problem; the plugin's n8n Workflow page shows the versions after a test ping.

Support covers the plugin and the n8n workflow template it ships: installing, connecting n8n, using the features, and bugs. It does not cover running your n8n instance, your AI provider account, your hosting, or custom changes to the workflow, though a pointer in the right direction is always offered.

## Security problems

Report a security problem privately, never on a public forum: write to hello@postquality.com.
