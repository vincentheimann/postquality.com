FAQ

What else do I pay?

Your own n8n hosting, and Anthropic per review, on your own API key. Both bill you directly: the price of Pro covers the plugin only. To cap the AI bill, set a spend limit in your Anthropic account. What it costs.

Is tax included?

No. Prices exclude VAT or sales tax; Freemius adds it at checkout where it applies. As the seller of record, Freemius also sends the invoice.

How do I cancel?

Cancel the renewal any time in the customer portal, linked from your purchase email. Pro stays on until the end of the year you paid for, then your site returns to Free. Until you cancel, Pro renews every year at the price you paid, and Freemius emails you before each renewal.

Within 14 days of buying or renewing, you can ask for a full refund instead: see the refund policy.

What counts as a site?

Each production site uses one activation, yours or a client's, and the license stays yours. Staging and local copies are free when Freemius recognises the address, such as localhost, .local, .test or a staging. subdomain, and you can move an activation in the customer portal. The full wording is in the terms of service, section 3.

Can one n8n serve several sites?

Yes. The setup guide gives each site its own workflow.

What happens when my license ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your site returns to Free: reviews, settings, schedules and role rights are kept, and Review and Key Takeaways keep working. Renewing unlocks everything again.
